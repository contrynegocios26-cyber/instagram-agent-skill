# Evidence that single-function consumer AI apps earn money in Brazil (2025-2026)

Research date: 2026-11-12. Method caveat that applies to every section: in this session the hosts sensortower.com, mobiletime.com.br, exame.com and apps.apple.com could not be opened (blocked by the session network policy), so every finding below comes from search-result extracts of those pages, not from a full read. Numbers are quoted as the extracts gave them; exact figures should be re-checked on the linked page before publishing. "Third-party estimate" = AppMagic / Sensor Tower / Appfigures / AppGoblin / AppRank model output, never audited store data.

## 1. Which AI apps rank in Brazil's top-grossing charts (overall and by category), 2025-2026

### Takeaway
The only AI app verifiably at the top of Brazil's grossing chart is ChatGPT (est. US$ 99 million in store billing in Brazil in 2025, #1 overall); the rest of the top 10 is Google, video streaming, CapCut, Canva and Tinder. Single-function AI apps do appear in category top-5 lists (Befit AI, YAZIO, Foodvisor in Health & Fitness) but at a scale of roughly US$ 10-40 thousand per week each, i.e. hundreds of thousands to low millions of dollars a year, not tens of millions.

### Cited Findings
Overall chart, Brazil, calendar 2025 (AppMagic estimates published by Mobile Time; store billing only, App Store + Google Play):
- ChatGPT was #1 in revenue in Brazil in 2025 with an estimated US$ 99 million. Followed by YouTube (US$ 61M), Google One (US$ 57M), CapCut (US$ 49M), Canva (US$ 42M), Globoplay (US$ 38M), HBO Max (US$ 33M), Gmail (US$ 29M), Tinder (US$ 28M), Google Fotos (US$ 25M). — [Mobile Time, 12 Jan 2026](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/noticias/12/01/2026/apps-mais-baixados-br-25/); [MacMagazine, 12 Jan 2026](https://macmagazine.com.br/post/2026/01/12/tiktok-foi-o-app-mais-baixado-no-brasil-em-2025-chatgpt-foi-o-que-gerou-mais-receita/)
- Methodology note from Mobile Time: figures cover only revenue that passes through store billing, mostly premium subscriptions; ad revenue is excluded. — [Mobile Time, 12 Jan 2026](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/noticias/12/01/2026/apps-mais-baixados-br-25/)
- Monthly view: ChatGPT led Brazil revenue with an estimated US$ 9.4 million in August 2025; four Google apps were in the top 10; Globoplay was the only Brazilian app in the list. — [Mobile Time, Sep/Oct 2025 monthly ranking](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/noticias/13/10/2025/dez-apps-com-maior-faturamento-2509/); [Paraíba Business](https://paraibabusiness.com.br/chatgpt-lidera-ranking-de-aplicativos-mais-baixados-e-faturamento-no-brasil-em-agosto/)

Education and Reference, Brazil:

- Reference: Hallow's Brazil revenue averaged about US$ 5.6K per week in Q3 2024 (older datapoint, pre-2025). — [Sensor Tower, Q3 2024 Reference Brazil](https://sensortower.com/blog/2024-q3-unified-top-5-reference%20apps-units-br-60772727241bc16eb8784bc8)
- AppBrain's top-grossing Reference chart for Brazil lists several Bible apps; rank only. — [AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com/stats/appstore-rankings/top_grossing/reference/br)

### Inferences
- Annualising the Sensor Tower weekly figures (my arithmetic, 52 weeks): Strava ~US$ 4-4.7M/yr and Flo ~US$ 2.9-3.3M/yr on iOS Brazil; Befit AI ~US$ 0.45-0.7M/yr on iOS Brazil; YAZIO ~US$ 0.8-2M/yr unified; Foodvisor ~US$ 0.65M/yr at its December peak. That is the realistic ceiling evidence for a category leader that is not ChatGPT: low single-digit millions of dollars a year, and an AI single-function app in a category top five earns around half a million dollars a year.
- ChatGPT alone (US$ 99M) is roughly 6% of Brazil's whole app-store spend if the 2024 total of US$ 1.7B is used as denominator (different years, indicative only). AI is clearly monetising in Brazil, but the verified money is concentrated in the general-purpose assistant.
- No single-function AI app (calorie, Bible, homework, headshot, identifier) appears in any Brazilian overall top-10 grossing list found for 2025.

### Gaps
- No 2026 monthly top-grossing list for Brazil was found (Mobile Time's 2026 AppMagic posts surfaced only download rankings).
- No Brazil top-grossing data found for Lifestyle or Productivity categories, and no revenue-ranked Education top five for 2025-2026.
- Sensor Tower category pages could not be opened; rank order within each top five is as listed in the extract, not a confirmed revenue ranking.

For Cal AI, Bible Chat, Gauth, Photomath, Question AI, Speak, Praktika, Remini, identifier apps, looks/skin apps, Quittr-style and astrology/tarot apps, no Brazil-specific revenue figure was found.

### Inferences
- The pattern for imported apps is "Brazil is a top-2 market by users, a small market by revenue": Duolingo (2nd market, 70M downloads), Hallow (2nd market, 4M+ downloads but only ~US$ 5.6K/week in Brazil in Q3 2024 against ~US$ 2.5M/month globally), Bible Chat (95%+ of revenue from the US). Volume is proven; monetisation per user is weak.
- For calorie counting, the Brazilian leaders in store revenue are European incumbents (YAZIO, Foodvisor) earning tens of thousands of dollars per week, which shows the niche pays in Brazil but at a modest level; Cal AI itself has no demonstrated Brazilian revenue.

### Gaps
- No Brazil-specific revenue or download data found for: Cal AI, Bible Chat, Gauth, Photomath, Question AI, Turbo AI, Brainly (app), Speak, Praktika, ELSA, Duolingo Max, Remini, Photoroom, headshot apps, plant/coin/rock identifiers, looks/skin/hair apps (Umax-style), Quittr-style habit apps, dream/astrology/tarot apps. Searches returned only global or US figures. Do not infer Brazilian revenue from the US numbers.
- Sensor Tower app-overview pages for Duolingo Brazil surfaced "last month" revenue values of US$ 11M-33M that are inconsistent with each other and with Brazil's total market; they look like global figures or page artefacts and were discarded.
- Glorify and Hallow Brazil user counts are from 2022-2024 and company-sourced; no 2025-2026 update found.

## 3. Brazilian-made single-function AI apps: reported revenue, downloads, funding

### Takeaway
Many Brazilian clones already exist in the two most obvious niches (photo calorie counting, mostly delivered over WhatsApp, and AI essay correction / exam prep for ENEM, concursos and OAB), but none has publicly reported revenue, audited user numbers or a funding round. The supply side is crowded with small unverified products; there is no documented Brazilian winner.

### Cited Findings
Nutrition / calorie counting by photo (all figures from the companies' own sites or store listings, unverified):
- Olho no Prato (iPhone app): monthly R$ 9.90, annual R$ 49.90, 3-day free trial. Publisher not identified; Brazilian origin is probable, not confirmed. — [App Store Brasil](https://apps.apple.com/br/app/id6757802677)
- Wellmi ("Mia", WhatsApp assistant): markets recognition of Brazilian dishes "from açaí to feijoada" and accuracy based on Brazilian academic food tables. — [wellmi.com.br](https://wellmi.com.br/)

- Others with the same proposition: Meu Nutri, SaudeAdvisor, FitSnap AI, SnackTrack, LucyFit. — [iameunutri.com.br](https://iameunutri.com.br/); [saudeadvisor.com.br](https://www.saudeadvisor.com.br/); [fitsnapia.com.br](https://fitsnapia.com.br/); [snacktrack.com.br](https://snacktrack.com.br/); [lucyfit.com.br](https://lucyfit.com.br/)

Faith in Portuguese:

### Inferences
- In Brazil the Cal AI idea has mostly been rebuilt as a WhatsApp bot rather than a store app. That fits an Android-heavy, low-ARPU market (no store fee, Pix billing, no install friction), and it means store charts under-count local competition.
- Local price anchors are far below imported apps: R$ 9.90/month and R$ 49.90/year (Olho no Prato) or R$ 29.90/month (IAprova, Redaciona) versus R$ 24.90-129.90/month for Bible Chat and R$ 199.90/year for Hallow.
- Absence of any press-covered funding round or revenue disclosure among roughly 20 named local products suggests these are micro-businesses; nobody has yet built the recognised "Cal AI of Brazil".

### Gaps
- No founder names, revenue, paying-subscriber counts or funding found for any of the Brazilian products above. Store-listing pages could not be opened to check rating counts as a proxy for traction.
- No Brazilian-made AI English tutor, identifier, looks or habit app with reported numbers was found.

## 4. What Brazilians pay: price points, revenue per download, Brazil's rank, Android vs iOS

### Takeaway
Brazil is a top-3 or top-4 country by downloads but only around 11th by consumer spend (about US$ 1.7 billion in store spending in 2024, roughly US$ 0.18 per download and US$ 11.6 per person per year), with Android on roughly 81-85% of phones. Subscription price points that exist in the market run from about R$ 10-30 per month and R$ 50-200 per year.

### Cited Findings
Market size and rank:
- Brazil's app market generated US$ 1.7 billion in consumer spending in 2024, up from US$ 1.4 billion in 2023; about 9.6 billion downloads in 2024 (second straight annual decline); average spend US$ 11.6 per person; 150M+ smartphone users; 4th largest market by downloads; Google One the top-grossing app. — [Business of Apps, Brazil App Market Statistics (2026)](https://www.businessofapps.com/data/brazil-app-market/)
- Brazil was the 3rd country by downloads in 2024 (Sensor Tower). — [MacMagazine, 23 Jan 2025](https://macmagazine.com.br/post/2025/01/23/sensor-tower-brasil-foi-o-terceiro-pais-que-mais-baixou-apps-em-2024/)
- A 2023 analysis ranked Brazil 11th worldwide in user spending on apps (US$ 1.7 billion, +26.2%) and 4th in downloads (10.2 billion). Note the same US$ 1.7B is attributed to 2023 here and to 2024 by Business of Apps. — [Mirian Gasparin, Apr 2024](https://miriangasparin.com.br/2024/04/brasileiros-gastam-r-17-bi-em-aplicativos-e-pais-segue-no-4o-posto-em-downloads-no-mundo/); contradicted on year by [Business of Apps](https://www.businessofapps.com/data/brazil-app-market/)
- For scale: US consumers spent nearly US$ 60 billion in apps in 2025; global in-app purchase revenue was US$ 167 billion in 2025, and non-game apps out-earned games for the first time. — [Consumidor Moderno](https://consumidormoderno.com.br/aplicativos-jogos-games-ia-generativa/); [PR Newswire / Sensor Tower](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/boosted-by-gen-ai-services-consumers-spent-more-money-in-apps-than-games-for-first-time-302665961.html)
- Brazil was the 4th largest market for app user-acquisition spend in 2024 at US$ 2.85 billion (AppsFlyer), behind the US, India and the UK; iOS user-acquisition in Brazil grew 168% in 2024. — [AppsFlyer](https://www.appsflyer.com/resources/reports/state-app-marketing-brazil-report/); [MacMagazine, 27 May 2025](https://macmagazine.com.br/post/2025/05/27/aquisicao-de-usuarios-em-apps-para-ios-no-brasil-cresceu-168-em-2024/)

Platform split:
- Android holds about 85% of Brazil's ~220 million smartphones and iOS about 14% (2025 blog-level source); another site gives 81.45% Android. — [RZ1](https://rz1.com.br/tecnologia/android-vs-ios-em-2025-uma-disputa-por-4-bilhoes-de-usuarios/801/); [CommandLinux](https://commandlinux.com/android/android-global-market-share-statistics/)
- Globally the App Store generated US$ 117.6 billion in consumer spending in 2025 versus US$ 49.2 billion for Google Play (Business of Apps, cited by Quash). — [Quash](https://quashbugs.com/blog/ios-vs-android-market-share-statistics)

Subscription benchmarks (RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026, 2025 data):
- Median year-one realised LTV per paying user: US$ 32 North America, US$ 25 Western Europe, US$ 23 global, US$ 14 India/Southeast Asia. No Latin America median was surfaced. Download-to-paid conversion: 2.6% North America versus 1.4% India/SEA. — [Mobile Marketing Reads summary](https://www.mobilemarketingreads.com/?p=50360); [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)
- In Latin America weekly plans capture 29% of subscription revenue (Adapty data via a third-party blog). — [AppsOps](https://appsops.store/blog/revenuecat-data-local-pricing)

- IAprova and Redaciona AI: R$ 29.90 per month; FoodSnap from R$ 5 in the first month; Lampejo R$ 6.99 per month on annual. — [iaprova.app](https://iaprova.app/home); [redacionaai.com.br](https://www.redacionaai.com.br/educacao); [foodsnap.com.br](https://www.foodsnap.com.br/); [Terra](https://www.terra.com.br/byte/como-app-brasileiro-corrige-redacao-do-enem-em-menos-de-30-segundos,2b84974e269b2f3d5369058c1909bd6exizczq46.html)

### Inferences
- Revenue per download in Brazil is about US$ 0.18 (US$ 1.7B divided by 9.6B downloads, 2024, my arithmetic). A US comparison could not be computed from sources found here because no US download count was retrieved; US spend alone (~US$ 60B in 2025) is about 35 times Brazil's total with a population only about 1.6 times larger.
- With iOS at roughly 14-19% of devices, an iOS-only Cal AI-style launch addresses a small slice of Brazilians, although that slice is the higher-spending one. An Android version, or a WhatsApp/Pix channel outside the stores, is needed for volume. This is consistent with what local founders are actually shipping (section 3).
- A realistic consumer price band for a localised single-function AI app is R$ 10-30 per month or R$ 50-200 per year. Bible Chat's R$ 129.90/month "Elite" tier shows foreign apps also test high anchors in Brazil, but there is no evidence of how many Brazilians pay it.

### Gaps
- No verified iOS vs Google Play revenue split for Brazil was found, and no 2025 full-year Brazil consumer-spend total (Sensor Tower's "State of Mobile 2026 Brasil" report exists at sensortower.com/report/state-of-mobile-brazil but could not be opened).
- No Brazil or Latin America RLTV, trial-conversion or refund figure from RevenueCat was retrievable.
- Brazilian price points for Cal AI, Gauth, Photomath, Speak, Praktika, Remini, Quittr and identifier apps were not obtained (App Store pages blocked).
- Weekly-plan pricing in R$ for any of these apps was not found.

## 5. Niches with clear Brazilian demand but no dominant localised AI app

### Takeaway
The demand side is large and documented in four areas (faith, exam prep, weight loss with GLP-1 drugs, English learning), and in each one the incumbent is either a foreign app monetising Brazilians weakly or a swarm of small local products with no visible leader. The weight-loss niche has the strongest fresh tailwind: the GLP-1 market roughly doubled in 2026 after semaglutide generics arrived.

### Cited Findings
Faith:
- Census 2022 (IBGE, released 6 June 2025): evangelicals rose from 21.6% (2010) to 26.9% of the population aged 10+, about 47.4 million people; Catholics fell from 65.1% to 56.7%; the country is about 87% Christian. Absolute totals vary by source (one table gives 66.8 million evangelicals), percentages are consistent. — [Correio Braziliense](https://www.correiobraziliense.com.br/brasil/2025/06/7167264-a-fe-brasileira-esta-mais-diversa-aponta-ibge.html); [Agência Gov](https://agenciagov.ebc.com.br/noticias/202506/censo-2022-catolicos-seguem-em-queda-evangelicos-e-sem-religiao-crescem-no-pais); [TNH1](https://www.tnh1.com.br/noticia/nid/novos-dados-do-ibge-revelam-um-brasil-87-cristao-ateus-triplicam/); conflicting absolute number in [Propmark](https://propmark.com.br/?p=123364)
- Foreign faith apps already treat Brazil as a top market: Hallow (2nd market, 4M+ downloads), Glorify (5M users claimed in 2022). — [Exame](https://exame.com/tecnologia/com-aporte-de-us-100-mi-e-apoio-do-vice-dos-eua-app-cristao-aposta-no-brasil-para-crescer/); [Comunhão](https://comunhao.com.br/glorify-alcanca-cinco-milhoes-de-usuarios-no-brasil/)

Exam prep:
- ENEM 2025 had 4,811,338 confirmed registrants (INEP, 23 July 2025), up 11.22% year on year; about 70% attended both exam days (9 and 16 November 2025). — [CNN Brasil](https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/educacao/enem-2025-inep-confirma-mais-de-48-milhoes-de-inscritos/); [O Povo](https://www.opovo.com.br/noticias/brasil/2025/11/16/enem-2025-cerca-de-70-dos-inscritos-fizeram-as-duas-etapas-da-prova.html)
- At least ten Brazilian AI essay-correction / exam products exist with prices around R$ 7-30 per month and no disclosed traction (section 3). — [iaprova.app](https://iaprova.app/home); [nota1000.app](https://nota1000.app/)

Weight loss / GLP-1:
- UBS BB projects Brazil's "weight-loss pen" market going from R$ 11 billion in 2025 to R$ 20 billion in 2026 with generics after the semaglutide patent expired in March 2026. — [Bloomberg Línea](https://www.bloomberglinea.com.br/negocios/mercado-de-canetas-emagrecedoras-deve-chegar-a-r-20-bi-no-brasil-com-genericos/)
- Another report: about R$ 10 billion in 2025 (already 4% of the pharmaceutical market), at least R$ 50 billion by 2030 with around 15 million users (projection). — [ISTOÉ Dinheiro](https://istoedinheiro.com.br/canetas-emagrecedoras-mercado-brasil-712026)
- Anvisa approved the first semaglutide generic (EMS); on 10 November 2026 it registered two more semaglutide pens (Ávita Care), bringing 2026 GLP-1 pen registrations to 22. — [Meio & Mensagem](https://www.meioemensagem.com.br/marketing/brasil-ganha-novas-marcas-de-canetas-emagrecedoras); [CREMERJ News, 11 Nov 2026](https://news.cremerj.org.br/2026/11/11/anvisa-registra-duas-novas-canetas-emagrecedoras-de-semaglutida/)

English learning:
- Duolingo has 70 million downloads in Brazil, its 2nd market, run by one local employee. — [ISTOÉ Dinheiro](https://istoedinheiro.com.br/brasil-duolingo-2o-mercado-apenas-uma)

General AI appetite:
- Generative-AI apps took 3 of the top 4 download positions in Brazil in April 2026, and ChatGPT reached 48 million Brazilian installs in 2025. — [Mobile Time, 19 May 2026](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/noticias/19/05/2026/apps-mais-baixados-2604/); [Mobile Time, 12 Jan 2026](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/noticias/12/01/2026/apps-mais-baixados-br-25/)

### Inferences
- GLP-1 companion (dose log, protein and calorie tracking by photo with Brazilian food, side-effect diary): demand is rising fast (R$ 11B to R$ 20B in one year, cheap generics widening the user base) and no localised app was found addressing it; local calorie products position as generic "nutritionist on WhatsApp". This looks like the least-contested angle with the newest tailwind, but the absence of a competitor in my searches is weak evidence, not proof of an empty niche.
- Evangelical-specific AI devotional/chat in Portuguese: 47 million evangelicals, yet the visible players are Catholic (Hallow, Bíblia Católica IA) or translated US/Romanian products (Bible Chat, Glorify). No Brazilian evangelical AI app with reported traction surfaced. The risk is that Brazilian faith users have so far produced large download numbers and small store revenue.
- ENEM/concurso/OAB: demand is proven (4.8M ENEM registrants a year) and seasonal, but supply is already fragmented across many similar R$ 30/month tools; the open position is a brand leader, not a missing product.
- English speaking tutor: Duolingo's reach shows huge top-of-funnel interest, and Speak/Praktika/ELSA have no documented Brazilian localisation push in consumer (ELSA is B2B-led). No Brazilian AI speaking tutor with numbers was found.

### Gaps
- No search-volume (Google Trends/Keyword Planner) or TikTok/Instagram hashtag data was collected for any niche; the demand signals above are population and market-size proxies only.
- No count of concurso candidates (e.g. CNU registrants) or OAB exam takers was retrieved.
- No current number of GLP-1 users in Brazil was found (only the 15 million projection for 2030).
- No Brazilian demand evidence was found for identifier apps, looks/skin/hair apps, Quittr-style habit apps or dream/astrology/tarot apps.

## 6. Documented cases of Brazilian indie developers or creators earning from such apps

### Takeaway
No verified case of a Brazilian indie developer earning meaningful revenue from a single-function AI subscription app was found. The few Brazilian numbers in circulation are self-reported, come from newsletters or course sales pages, and concern finance or web SaaS products rather than Cal AI-style apps.

### Cited Findings
- Visor Finance (founder Gabriel Packer; personal-finance app, not single-function AI): 3 thousand sign-ups in three days crashed the platform at launch, and it closed its first month with R$ 70 thousand in revenue. Self-reported via a Substack newsletter in 2026; first-month figure, not sustained MRR; he has a design agency, a mobile developer and support staff. — [Kamelo (Substack)](https://kamelo.substack.com/p/o-app-que-caiu-no-dia-do-lancamento)
- Automarticles (AI blog-article SaaS, web, not mobile): R$ 199 per month and "over 600 paying clients", implying about R$ 130 thousand per month. Unverified, from a marketing blog. — [IBE blog](https://blog.ibe.ia.br/blog/como-criar-um-saas-com-ia/)
- App Builders (Enzo Segatto), a paid "build your first app in 48h" immersion, advertises R$ 941,000 billed per month on its sales page. Self-reported marketing claim from a course seller; treat as unverified. — [appbuilders.com.br](https://appbuilders.com.br/)
- Subfaro: Brazilian solo developer built a subscription-tracking app in about 10 days and posted a build log on TabNews; no revenue reported, Android just launched and iOS pending. — [TabNews](https://www.tabnews.com.br/subfaro/pitch-fiz-sozinho-um-app-de-assinaturas-em-10-dias-o-que-aprendi)
- The stories circulating in Brazilian media about young founders making millions from an AI app refer to Cal AI's American founder, not to Brazilians. — [Capitalist](https://capitalist.com.br/o-jovem-de-18-anos-que-criou-um-app-de-ia-e-ja-fatura-milhoes-por-mes/); [Money Times](https://www.moneytimes.com.br/como-baixar-o-app-que-conta-as-calorias-do-seu-prato-com-uma-simples-foto-e-deixou-o-fundador-virar-milionario-mlem/)

### Inferences
- The Cal AI narrative has reached Brazil mainly as content and as a course-selling hook ("build an app in 48h"), not yet as documented local outcomes. An entrepreneur should weight the loudest Brazilian revenue claims accordingly, since the largest one found comes from a party selling training.
- The lack of public Brazilian success cases is consistent with sections 1-3: store revenue in Brazil for this category is real but small per app, and local builders gravitate to WhatsApp/Pix products whose revenue never shows up in store-intelligence tools.

### Gaps
- No interviews, podcasts, YouTube videos or X/Twitter threads with Brazilian founders disclosing RevenueCat/App Store screenshots for a single-function AI app were found; X, YouTube and Portuguese-language communities are poorly covered by the search tool used, so this is an absence in search results, not proof that no such case exists.
- No "Indie Hackers Brasil" community datapoints were retrieved.
