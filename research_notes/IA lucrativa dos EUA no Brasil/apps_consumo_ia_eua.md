> Research date: 2026-11-12. Numbers are labelled **[company]** (company/acquirer/CEO statement), **[estimate]** (Sensor Tower / Appfigures / AppMagic / Sacra / Latka third-party estimate), or **[self-reported/secondary]** (founder interviews, podcasts, blogs, aggregators). ## 1. Which consumer AI app categories earn the most in the US?

### Takeaway
Money is extremely concentrated: general assistants (ChatGPT) take over two-thirds of generative-AI in-app spending, and below that the proven nine-figure consumer lines are AI music (Suno), AI personal finance with cash advances (Cleo), AI language tutoring (Speak), AI photo editing (Remini, Photoroom), note-taking (Plaud, Otter) and faith (Hallow). The "small-team, replicable" tier ($5M-$50M/yr) is calorie tracking (Cal AI), study/note tools (Turbo AI), habit/quit apps (Quittr), looks-rating (Umax), AI Bible chat and the long tail of AI companions.

### Inferences
- Rough revenue ranking of categories outside general chatbots, from the figures above: music (Suno $300M+) and finance (Cleo ~$400M, but really a lending business) > note-taking hardware/software (Plaud, Otter ~$100M+ each) > language (Speak ~$60-150M) > photo (Remini ~$93M, Photoroom $65-94M) > companions (whole category ~$120M in 2025, ~$330M in H1 2026 if both halves of the Appfigures split are added — my arithmetic) > faith (Hallow ~$40M) > calorie tracking (Cal AI $30-40M) > study, habit, looks ($5-15M each).
- Suno's implied revenue per subscriber is about $150/year, or $12.50/month ($300M / 2M; my arithmetic).
- Difficulty to build (my assessment, not sourced): LOW (wrapper on a vision/LLM API plus onboarding quiz and paywall) — calorie photo trackers, looks rating, dating-reply helpers, Bible chat, habit/quit apps, basic study note apps. MEDIUM — companions (memory, moderation, voice, inference cost), language tutoring (speech pipeline, curriculum), photo editors (model cost, quality bar set by Remini/CapCut). HIGH — music generation (own models, licensing lawsuits), fintech like Cleo (credit licences, capital), hardware like Plaud, kids toys (hardware plus child-safety regulation).
- The best match for a small Brazilian team is the LOW tier, where the moat is distribution (short-video creators), not technology. The high-revenue names (Suno, Cleo, Plaud, Speak) are venture-scale and not copyable cheaply.

### Gaps
- Exact subscription prices for Cal AI, Umax, Turbo AI, Character.AI, Replika, Speak, Photoroom and Remini were not captured in the sources reached; only Quittr ($30/yr), Hallow ($69.99/yr), YourMove ($12.99/mo) and Curio ($99) had sourced prices.
- No revenue data found for AI headshot generators, AI video generators (as consumer apps), astrology AI apps, AI kids apps, AI skin/fitness apps, or AI resume/job tools.
- No US-only revenue split per category; most estimates are global.
- ElevenLabs/voice apps and Duolingo's AI tiers were not researched.

## 2. Revenue of standout small-team apps and their playbook

### Takeaway
The repeatable small-team playbook is: a single-feature AI utility for a young audience, built in days or weeks on third-party models, sold through a hard paywall after a long onboarding quiz, and distributed by paying many small TikTok/Instagram creators and then bringing UGC in-house. Most revenue numbers in this tier are founder self-reported; Cal AI ($30M+, acquirer statement) and Turbo AI ($13M lifetime, press) are the best-evidenced.

### Inferences
- Founder attribution for Umax conflicts across sources (Blake Anderson vs Sarthak Dhawan); the Anderson attribution is supported by more sources. Treat any single Umax number as approximate.
- The same small network (the "App Mafia" circle: Anderson, Yadegari, McLaren, Slater) recycled one template across calorie, looks, dating and quit-porn niches; the transferable asset is the creator-marketing machine, which can be rebuilt with Brazilian creators at lower cost per video.
- None of these apps owns its AI model; inference is a per-use cost, so a Brazilian clone priced in reais must watch gross margin on vision/LLM calls.

### Gaps
- No verified solo-founder example at scale: every app above has at least two founders or a team by the time revenue was reported.
- Marketing spend, CAC and profit margins are not disclosed for any of these apps.
- Cal AI's later revenue (after Dec 2025) and whether growth continued under MyFitnessPal: not found.

## 3. What app-store intelligence says (Sensor Tower, Appfigures, AppMagic, RevenueCat, a16z)

### Takeaway
AI apps sell faster and at higher prices than other subscription apps but lose subscribers about 30% faster: roughly four in five annual subscribers do not renew. Spending is concentrated in a few products and a few heavy payers.

### Cited Findings
- RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026 (March 2026): sample of 115,000+ apps and $16B+ revenue; 27.1% of subscription apps are AI-powered — [SaaStr](https://www.saastr.com/the-top-10-learnings-from-revenuecats-state-of-subscription-apps/); [TechCrunch, Mar 10 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/10/ai-powered-apps-struggle-with-long-term-retention-new-report-shows)
- AI apps earn about 39-41% more realized lifetime value per payer but churn ~30% faster — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/ai-apps-earn-41-more-per-user-but-churn-30-faster-revenuecat-finds/)
- Retention: annual-plan retention 21.1% for AI apps vs 30.7% for non-AI; monthly-plan figure reported as 6.1% vs 9.5% (appears to be 12-month retention of monthly plans); months 3-6 are where AI apps lose most subscribers — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/10/ai-powered-apps-struggle-with-long-term-retention-new-report-shows); [Creem](https://www.creem.io/blog/ai-app-retention-paradox-churn-2026)
- Trial-to-paid: one summary gives a median 8.5% for AI apps vs 5.6% non-AI (single secondary source) — [AI Productivity](https://aiproductivity.ai/news/ai-apps-earn-more-churn-faster-revenuecat-2026/)
- Trials are getting shorter: share of apps with trials of 4 days or less rose from 42.1% (2025) to 46.5% (2026) — [SaaStr](https://www.saastr.com/the-top-10-learnings-from-revenuecats-state-of-subscription-apps/)
- Growth is concentrating in the top apps — [Subscription Insider](https://www.subscriptioninsider.com/article-type/news/revenuecat-data-shows-subscription-app-growth-concentrating-at-the-top)
- a16z Top 100, 7th edition (Oct 5 2026): first edition with US card-spend data (YipitData); the top 1% of payers account for 19.5% of consumer AI spend, more than the bottom 50% combined (16.6%); 7.3% of Claude's consumer payers are on the $100/month plan vs 1.3% for Google and 1.1% for ChatGPT; only 11 newcomers, the fewest ever; NSFW products removed from the ranking (they were over 20% of list web traffic); 9 of 15 major consumer categories (incl. travel, jobs, social, dating) have no standalone AI product in the top 100 — [a16z](https://a16z.com/100-gen-ai-apps-7/); [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/a16z-top-100-consumer-ai-apps-seventh-edition/); [ToolNavs](https://toolnavs.com/article/2319-a16z-top-100-consumer-ai-apps-7th-edition-only-11-newcomers-but-paying-users-are)

- Sensor Tower and Appfigures category numbers are in section 1.

### Inferences
- With ~21% annual renewal, the business is closer to selling a one-year product than a subscription; small-team apps survive on a constant flow of new installs, which is why they depend on viral short video.
- Annual plans charged upfront (Quittr $30/yr, Hallow $69.99/yr, Speak >50% annual) are the standard response to churn; in Brazil this collides with lower card limits and the habit of paying monthly or by Pix, so conversion on annual plans may be weaker (my assessment).

### Gaps
- The primary RevenueCat report and the a16z pages could not be opened; the figures come from secondary summaries and should be checked before publication.
- No AI-specific price-point benchmarks (median monthly/annual price) or refund rates were captured.
- No Sensor Tower list of top-grossing AI apps in the US beyond ChatGPT was found.

## 4. Categories that collapsed, churn heavily, or face platform/regulatory risk

### Takeaway
The riskiest categories are AI therapy (state bans, FDA cost, Woebot's closure) and AI companions (new laws, age gating, heavy inference costs — Character.AI was losing about $8M a month in 2026). All thin wrappers share two structural risks: high churn and ChatGPT's dominance of AI spending.

### Cited Findings
- AI therapy: Illinois banned AI systems from providing therapy or being advertised as therapy without a licensed professional (signed Aug 2025; $10,000 fine per violation); Ash stopped operating in Illinois; other states are reported to be following — [Axios Chicago](https://www.axios.com/local/chicago/2025/08/06/illinois-ai-therapy-ban-mental-health-regulation); [Quartz](https://qz.com/illinois-banned-ai-therapy-crackdowns-3-more-states-next)
- Woebot shut down for lack of a business model and FDA authorization cost — [Telehealth.org](https://telehealth.org/news/ai-psychotherapy-shutdown-what-woebots-exit-signals-for-clinicians/)
- Companions: California SB 243 (signed Oct 13 2025, in force Jan 1 2026) requires clear AI disclosure and extra protections for known minors, with damages of at least $1,000 per violation — [ZwillGen](https://www.zwillgen.com/artificial-intelligence/california-takes-lead-on-ai-companion-chatbot-regulation/)
- Apple reportedly requires AI companion apps to be rated 18+ on the App Store **[single secondary source]** — [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/ai-news/apple-app-store-ai-companions/)
- Apple App Review guideline 5.1.2(i) (Nov 2025) now requires apps to disclose and get explicit permission before sharing personal data with third-party AI — affects every wrapper that sends photos or chats to an external model — [iGeeksBlog](https://www.igeeksblog.com/apple-app-store-ai-data-disclosure-guidelines/); [Cult of Mac](https://www.cultofmac.com/news/apple-app-review-guidelines-on-ai)
- Character.AI: about $5.3M monthly revenue against a reported ~$8M monthly loss (Aug 2026), turned to advertising (47% of revenue); coverage refers to a bankruptcy — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/character-ai-loses-8m-a-month-despite-2-5m-in-monthly-ad-sales/); [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/?p=4327538)
- Replika estimates trend downward (~$30M in 2023, ~$24M or $14M in 2024, far lower store estimates in 2026) and it received a €5M GDPR fine — [Latka](https://getlatka.com/companies/replika.ai); [AI Companion Pick](https://aicompanionpick.com/replika-user-base-2026)
- Companion revenue is winner-take-most: top 10% of apps take 89% — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/12/ai-companion-apps-on-track-to-pull-in-120m-in-2025)
- Faith: Hallow's Lent peak was flat year on year ($10M to $9.7M) — [Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/hallow-lent-surge-prayer-app-revenue)
- Kids: early evidence of children being disappointed by AI toys — [Welcome.ai](https://welcome.ai/content/childrens-disappointment-with-ai-toys-signals-market-risks-for-curio)
- Incumbent absorption: Cal AI was bought by the category incumbent MyFitnessPal, which also keeps its own AI features — [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/myfitnesspal-acquires-cal-ai-to-expand-ai-powered-nutrition-tracking-portfolio)
- ChatGPT concentration (over two-thirds of gen-AI IAP in Q2 2025) — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-ai-apps-market-overview-2025)

### Inferences
- Features most exposed to being absorbed by ChatGPT/Gemini are generic ones (homework answers, text rewriting, dating replies, resume writing); the apps that held revenue wrap the model in a habit loop or a specific job (log a meal, record a lecture, pray daily, count days sober).
- Dating helpers and resume tools show many apps and price points but no verified revenue and no presence on a16z's list — a sign of a crowded, low-retention category rather than a proven one.
- For Brazil, regulated niches (therapy, finance/credit, minors) add LGPD and sector-regulator exposure on top of the US-style risks (my assessment).

### Gaps
- Could not confirm the details of Character.AI's under-18 restrictions or the exact status of its bankruptcy from a primary source.
- No data on Google Play policy changes for AI apps.
- No hard churn data per category (only AI vs non-AI overall).

## 5. Revenue outside the US, and presence in Brazil / Latin America

### Takeaway
Hard data on Brazil exists only for the big assistants: ChatGPT was Brazil's top-grossing app in 2025 (about $99M) with 48M installs, proving Brazilians pay for AI. For the niche apps (Cal AI, Umax, Quittr, Bible Chat, Turbo AI) no country split was found, so their strength in Brazil is unknown.

### Cited Findings
- ChatGPT was the highest-revenue app in Brazil in 2025, with an estimated US$99M, and the third most downloaded (48M installs; TikTok first with 59M); Gemini was sixth with 27M **[AppMagic estimate]** — [MacMagazine](https://macmagazine.com.br/post/2026/01/12/tiktok-foi-o-app-mais-baixado-no-brasil-em-2025-chatgpt-foi-o-que-gerou-mais-receita/); [Mobile Time](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/noticias/12/01/2026/apps-mais-baixados-br-25/)
- ChatGPT was also the top-grossing app across Latin America in 2025 and third in downloads **[AppMagic]** — [Mobile Time](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/noticias/09/01/2026/chatgpt-receita-al-25/)
- In the first four months of 2026 ChatGPT led Brazil downloads with 18.94M; Gemini rose from 2.9M to 10.78M — [Mobile Time](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/?p=858662)
- Latin America's gen-AI IAP revenue grew 147% between H2 2024 and H1 2025, the fastest of the regions listed (Asia +136%, Middle East +131%, Europe +121%); ChatGPT leads revenue in every major market except mainland China **[Sensor Tower]** — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-ai-apps-market-overview-2025)
- Sensor Tower reports gen-AI IAP of $6.1B for Q2 2025-Q1 2026, +232% YoY, in its APAC AI report (scope of the figure — regional or global — unclear from the summary) — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-ai-apps-in-apac-2026-report)

### Inferences
- $99M a year from one AI subscription in Brazil shows real willingness to pay, but at ChatGPT's regionally adjusted pricing; US price points like $30-70/year for a single-purpose app likely need to be cut for Brazil (my assessment).
- Categories with natural Brazilian fit and no evidence of a localized leader in the sources: AI calorie tracking with Brazilian foods, AI Bible/faith chat in Portuguese (large evangelical and Catholic audience), exam study tools (ENEM/concursos), English speaking practice. These are hypotheses to be validated against Brazilian store charts, not findings.
- US-specific dependencies: Cleo (US credit/cash-advance regulation), Hallow-style celebrity Catholic marketing, college lecture culture for Turbo AI, US creator CPMs for the influencer playbook.

### Gaps
- No US vs international revenue split was found for any niche app; no evidence either way on whether Cal AI, Umax, Quittr, Bible Chat, Speak, Suno or Character.AI are strong in Brazil.
- No Brazilian store top-grossing list for AI apps beyond ChatGPT/Gemini was retrieved.
- Sensor Tower's US share of gen-AI IAP was not found.
