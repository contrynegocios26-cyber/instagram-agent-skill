# Low-capital AI business models in the United States, 2025-2026 (skeptical review)

Research date: 2026-11-12. Scope: models a solo founder or small team can start without venture funding.

**Method caveat for the report writer (read first).** Direct page fetches were blocked in this session (hosts such as bigideasdb.com, techstartups.com, thenextweb.com and beehiiv.com were outside the allowed network), so every finding below comes from search-result extracts, not from a full read of the page. Numbers are as quoted in those extracts. Three evidence grades are used throughout:

- **[VERIFIED]**: regulator filing, payment-processor-verified data, or a primary company announcement.
- **[SELF-REPORTED]**: founder or company claim, unaudited.
- **[VENDOR/MARKETING]**: published by a party that sells the tool, course or service in question.

No Business Insider, WSJ, Bloomberg or The Information article with documented small-AI-agency earnings surfaced in the searches. That absence is itself a finding: the "AI agency" income narrative is carried almost entirely by vendors and course sellers.

## 1. AI automation agencies (AAA) and AI consulting/implementation for small businesses

### Takeaway
What is sold is real (voice agents, chatbots, lead follow-up, n8n/Make/Zapier workflows on setup-plus-retainer pricing), but I found no independent dataset on agency earnings or failure rates. The only hard public evidence in this space is negative: the FTC case against Air AI, which sold the "AI agency/AI sales agent" opportunity with unsubstantiated earnings claims.

### Cited Findings
**Pricing (all VENDOR/MARKETING, 2026)**
- Single-purpose AI voice agent: $1,500-$5,000 setup plus $300-$800/month retainer, per one agency pricing guide (2026) — [Retell AI blog](https://www.retellai.com/blog/how-agencies-price-ai-voice-agents); a second guide quotes higher, $3,000-$8,000 setup and $1,000-$3,000/month — [Ciela, 2026-03-27](https://ciela.ai/blogs/ai-agency-pricing-guide-retainers-projects)
- Multi-agent systems (inbound + outbound + routing): $5,000-$25,000 setup plus $1,000-$3,000/month (2026) — [Layer3 Labs, updated 2026-07-02](https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/ai-agency-pricing)
- The setup fee is the most negotiable lever; "many operators discount setup to $1 or $0" in exchange for a 12-month retainer minimum (2026). This signals price pressure — [Ciela, 2026-01-08](https://ciela.ai/blogs/how-much-to-charge-for-ai-voice-agent)
- AI chatbots for local businesses are pitched at $1,000-$5,000 retainers (2026) — [Vantaige](https://vantaige.io/blog/sell-ai-chatbots-local-businesses-1k-5k-retainer-2026)
- Lower-end figures from side-hustle blogs: $200-$700 per automation setup; elsewhere $1,500-$8,000 per build and $1,500-$3,000/month maintenance; a one-off "AI audit" at $300-$1,500 (2026, unverified blogs citing each other) — [UC Strategies](https://ucstrategies.com/news/everyone-is-lying-about-ai-income-i-found-the-only-hustles-that-actually-make-5k-month/); [The Interview Guys](https://blog.theinterviewguys.com/best-side-hustles-from-home/)
- Running cost of voice agents: about $0.13-$0.33 per minute across common stacks (2026) — [Ringly](https://www.ringly.io/blog/voice-ai-pricing)

**Earnings evidence (weak)**
- A beginner-focused review calculated that a $500/month beginner automation retainer nets about $15.73/hour after tools and taxes (2026; one anecdotal calculation) — [Hustle Audit](https://hustleaudit.beehiiv.com/p/ai-automation-agency-beginner-review-2026)
- The same review cites a May 2026 analysis naming scope creep as the main margin killer (original analysis not located) — [Hustle Audit](https://hustleaudit.beehiiv.com/p/ai-automation-agency-beginner-review-2026)
- A roundup of Reddit threads says clients pay $500-$2,000/month for reliable automation and one operator reportedly reached $26,000/month (2026, secondhand, underlying threads not verified) — [Ciela, 2026-07-02](https://ciela.ai/blogs/vapi-ai-reddit-review-for-agencies)
- A blog attributes to a "HighLevel 2026 partner report" a figure of $2,000-$5,000/month in recurring retainers by month 12 for the average freelancer managing AI automation; the report itself could not be located (treat as unverified) — [LumiChats](https://lumichats.com/blog/ai-side-hustles-actually-make-money-2026-usa)
- One Mixergy interview is titled "I make $4.5 million implementing AI" (self-reported; date and details not extracted) — [Mixergy](https://mixergy.com/interviews/i-make-4-5-million-implementing-ai/amp/)
- Lead generation, not delivery, is described as the binding constraint: it "requires getting in front of business owners physically or through warm referrals" (2026) — [UC Strategies](https://ucstrategies.com/news/everyone-is-lying-about-ai-income-i-found-the-only-hustles-that-actually-make-5k-month/)

**Failure-rate evidence (none specific to agencies)**
- Commonly cited figures are about enterprise AI projects, not small agencies, and reached me only through blogs: RAND (more than 80% of AI projects fail), MIT Project NANDA (95% of enterprise gen-AI pilots show little or no measurable return), Gartner June 2025 (over 40% of agentic AI projects cancelled by end of 2027) — [Tyler E. Willis blog](https://tylerewillis.com/blog/95-percent-ai-pilots-no-roi-what-freelancers-agencies-must-do-differently); [Trillet](https://trillet.ai/blogs/is-the-ai-agency-bubble-real-what-the-data-says)

**Regulatory evidence [VERIFIED]**
- FTC sued Air AI on 2025-08-25, alleging deceptive claims about business growth, earnings potential and refund guarantees, and Business Opportunity Rule violations; it alleged some consumers, many of them small business owners, lost as much as $250,000 — [FTC press release, 2025-08](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/08/ftc-sues-stop-air-ai-using-deceptive-claims-about-business-growth-earnings-potential-refund)
- Settlement announced 2026-03-24: owners banned from marketing business opportunities; $18 million monetary judgment, largely suspended for inability to pay, with $50,000 actually payable — [FTC press release, 2026-03](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/03/air-ai-its-owners-will-be-banned-marketing-business-opportunities-settle-ftc-charges-company-misled)

### Inferences
- Realistic picture for a solo operator: a handful of clients at $300-$2,000/month each. Five-figure monthly revenue exists but only in self-reported or vendor-relayed form.
- Setup fees discounted to $0-$1 and vendor blogs saturating search results both point to a crowded, commoditizing low end in the US.
- Requirements for a replicator: capital under roughly $300/month in tools (vendor estimate, see section 2); skills are sales/outreach first, then workflow tooling; time to first revenue is not documented anywhere reliable. Failure modes named in sources: no client acquisition channel, scope creep, low retainers that do not cover support time.
- Hype share: very high. Nearly all quantitative claims originate from platform vendors or course sellers.

### Gaps
- No survey or dataset of AAA revenue, churn or survival exists in what I could reach.
- No mainstream-press investigation of AAA earnings found.
- Time to first revenue: no reliable data.

## 2. White-label / reseller models (AI receptionists, voice agents, chatbots, database reactivation, speed-to-lead)

### Takeaway
The clearest verified money in voice AI is being made by the platforms, not demonstrably by resellers. Reseller pricing ($250-$500+/month per receptionist) and margins (50-70%) are vendor claims with no independent confirmation.

### Cited Findings
**Platform revenue (shows where demand is; mostly third-party estimates)**
- Retell AI: about $60M annualized revenue in April 2026, up 650% year over year, on only $5.1M total funding (Sacra estimate) — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/retell-ai-60m-yr-up-650-yoy)
- Vapi: "healthy" eight-figure ARR at its latest round, $500M valuation per a May 2026 TechCrunch report (relayed secondhand) — [Sacra, Retell AI company page](https://sacra.com/c/retell-ai)
- ElevenLabs: ended 2025 around $350M ARR and passed $500M in the first four months of 2026 (includes a much broader audio business) — [Pulse](https://www.pulse.bot/ai/news/elevenlabs-ceo-says-the-voice-ai-startup-crossed-330-million-arr-last-year-a0d9ed64-ff67-4840-9172-50ed8972dbd9)
- GoHighLevel company-supplied 2025 figures: about 7 million AI voice calls, 179 million booked appointments, $5.2B in sales facilitated [VENDOR] — [SyncGTM review, 2026](https://syncgtm.com/blog/gohighlevel-review-2026)
- GoHighLevel plans: $97, $297 and $497/month; voice usage roughly $0.02-$0.08 per minute billed separately (2026) — [DEV Community pricing breakdown](https://dev.to/robert_denim/gohighlevel-pricing-2026-complete-cost-breakdown-hidden-fees-4cdm)

**Reseller economics [VENDOR/MARKETING]**
- Typical retail: $250-$500+/month per AI receptionist (2026) — [My AI Front Desk](https://llms.myaifrontdesk.com/white-label-reseller-agencies)
- Wholesale examples: Trillet agency plan $299/month plus $0.12/minute; Synthflow moved to pay-as-you-go plus a roughly $2,000/month white-label toolkit (2026) — [Trillet](https://trillet.ai/blogs/ai-receptionist-white-label-pricing)
- Claimed gross margins: above 60%, or a 50-70% target (2026, no independent data) — [Trillet](https://trillet.ai/blogs/whitelabel-guide)
- Cost stacking: wrapper dashboards (e.g. ChatDash, VoiceAIWrapper) sit on Vapi/Retell/Voiceflow, so the reseller pays wrapper fee plus provider subscription plus per-minute charges, and inherits the provider's outages and price changes (2026) — [Trillet](https://trillet.ai/blogs/voice-agent-reseller-program-comparison)
- Start-up cost estimated at under $300/month as of June 2026 (built around the vendor's own product) — [Trillet](https://trillet.ai/blogs/how-much-does-it-cost-to-start-an-ai-voice-agency)
- Retell offers white-label tokens that hide its branding, confirming a formal reseller channel — [Retell AI](https://www.retellai.com/use-cases/white-label-ai-voice-agents)

**Database reactivation / speed-to-lead**
- One Skool post describes a member reaching $1K MRR from a reactivation database (self-reported, single anecdote, date not extracted) — [Skool, AI Automation Insiders](https://chat.skool.com/ai-automation-insiders/i-made-1k-mrr-from-a-database-jay-called-garbage-and-ai-didnt-do-the-hard-part)

### Inferences
- Unit math on vendor numbers: $300-$500 retail minus about $0.12-$0.33/minute usage and a $299+ platform fee means break-even needs several clients; margin collapses for high-call-volume clients on flat pricing.
- The reseller has no moat: the same platforms sell direct, and end customers can buy a $97/month GoHighLevel plan themselves.
- Requirements: low capital (hundreds of dollars a month), no coding, but strong local sales ability. Brazil-specific note for the writer: voice quality in Portuguese, WhatsApp-first behavior and telephony costs would need separate validation; nothing in these sources covers it.
- Hype share: very high. Zero independent reseller earnings data found.

### Gaps
- No verified reseller income, churn or client-count distributions.
- No independent results data for database reactivation or speed-to-lead offers.
- GoHighLevel agency count and ARR not confirmed (one unverified profile says it passed $100M ARR — [Kustiq](https://kustiq.com/company/gohighlevel.com)).

## 3. Micro-SaaS and indie AI products

### Takeaway
This is the category with the best evidence, and that evidence says outcomes are extremely skewed: a few solo founders earn $100K+/month while the median AI product on a processor-verified listing earns almost nothing.

### Cited Findings
**Distribution data**
- TrustMRR pulls revenue from payment processors (not self-reported). One aggregator counts 6,040+ verified startups, average MRR $4,682; among 1,213 AI startups the **median is about $7 MRR**, attributed to thin wrappers (2026; aggregator sells access to this data) — [BigIdeasDB](https://bigideasdb.com/docs/trustmrr-overview)
- Among 3,787 micro-SaaS startups reporting revenue: median $145 MRR, average $4,298, only 6.1% above $10K MRR (2026; excludes zero-revenue projects) — [youngju.dev, 2026-05-14](https://www.youngju.dev/blog/culture/2026-05-14-micro-saas-case-studies-2026-one-person-business-economics-stack-deep-dive-2026.en)
- Community-survey estimate: 50% of actively building indie hackers sit at $0-$1K MRR; fewer than 5% cross $100K (approximate, 2026) — [Mean CEO blog](https://blog.mean.ceo/?p=11175)
- Individual verified listing example: a Stripe-verified product with 421 active subscriptions at about $1,705 MRR (2026) — [TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/fld)
- "Average AI wrapper has 65% churn within 90 days" appears with no methodology; do not rely on it — [Jack's newsletter](https://jacks-newsletter-0adf2e.beehiiv.com/p/how-an-ai-wrapper-hit-40k-mrr-and-what-it-means-for-our-tools-f717)

**Named cases (SELF-REPORTED unless noted)**
- Photo AI (Pieter Levels): about $105,000/month revenue and about $80,000/month profit as of March 2026, founder-reported; Levels notes it is monthly revenue, not MRR, because of annual plans — [Photo AI teardown](https://td-site-dx7.pages.dev/case-studies/photo-ai/)
- Third-party estimates put Photo AI at $132K-$138K MRR in November 2025; the two figures conflict, and an Indie Hackers post exists in both "$132k" and "$182k" versions — [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/how-one-photo-ai-app-generates-132k-monthly-after-70-failed-startups/); [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/photo-ai-by-pieter-levels-complete-deep-dive-case-study-0-to-132k-mrr-in-18-months-3a9a2b1579)
- Interior AI: about $38K-$45K/month, single source, unconfirmed — [youngju.dev](https://www.youngju.dev/blog/culture/2026-05-14-micro-saas-case-studies-2026-one-person-business-economics-stack-deep-dive-2026.en)
- fly.pieter.com: $87K MRR / $1M annualized in 17 days; a separate headline says it "then went to zero" — [Tilacum](https://tilacum.vercel.app/news/solo-founder-1m-arr-ai-coded-2026); [DEV Community](https://dev.to/promptway/he-built-a-flight-simulator-in-three-hours-and-hit-1m-a-year-in-17-days-then-it-went-to-zero-1b1l)
- HeadshotPro (Danny Postma) $3.6M ARR solo; Marc Lou $1.5M+ ARR from ShipFast and related products (2026 roundup) — [Mean CEO blog](https://blog.mean.ceo/top-solo-founder-startups/)
- Solo founder reporting about $1.03M ARR at the start of 2026 and about $125K MRR by June 2026 — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/hitting-125k-mrr-as-a-solo-founder-by-doubling-down-on-the-right-segment-c4o2Tfs6mjdpip5yZhaO)
- Leadmore AI (Reddit marketing tool) claims $1M ARR — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/i-built-a-reddit-marketing-product-leadmore-ai-and-it-just-crossed-1m-arr-f8b758eb1a)
- Cal AI: two-person team claiming $21M (headline only, unverified) — [Keenable](https://select.keenable.ai/r/lean-ai-products-at-1m-arr-2025-sep-2026/WwKmTVPhPPZWlOclJqrBSg)
- Base44: sources disagree on whether it counts as solo (one says solo developer Maor Shlomo; another lists 11 people, acquired by Wix) — [We Are Founders](https://www.wearefounders.uk/the-30-highest-valued-solo-startups-of-2026/)

**Exit multiples (weak)**
- SaaS on Acquire.com reportedly normalized at 3-5x profit (2025 findings, secondary summary) — [video summary](https://video-summaries.pages.dev/acquisition-multiples-report-2025-findings.html)
- One blog claims 2026 ranges of 2.5-5x ARR for traditional SaaS and 3x for commoditized GPT wrappers up to 15x+ for differentiated AI (no methodology) — [ExitBid](https://exitbid.pages.dev/blog/how-to-sell-ai-saas-business-2026)

### Inferences
- Winning niches in the named cases are narrow consumer or prosumer jobs with an obvious paid output (headshots, interior renders, calorie tracking, marketing tooling). Most successes belong to founders with large pre-existing audiences, which is a distribution advantage a newcomer does not have.
- Requirements: capital is low (API and hosting costs), but coding or strong AI-assisted building skill and a distribution channel are required. Time to first revenue is not documented; the median outcome is near zero.
- Failure modes: thin wrapper replaced by a model-provider feature, no buyers, novelty spikes that collapse (fly.pieter.com).
- Hype share: medium. Real verified data exists, but public attention concentrates on outliers.

### Gaps
- TrustMRR's own primary statistics page could not be read; dataset size is reported inconsistently (840+ at API launch versus 6,040+).
- No current Acquire.com or Flippa AI-specific multiples report found.
- Starter Story cases were not retrieved.

## 4. AI content businesses (faceless video, AI UGC ads, newsletters, programmatic SEO, e-commerce/print-on-demand)

### Takeaway
Platform risk became the dominant fact of 2025-2026: YouTube, Google Search, Etsy and (less clearly) TikTok all moved against mass-produced AI content. The verified winners here are tool vendors (Arcads, HeyGen) and large media brands, not small operators.

### Cited Findings
**Faceless YouTube**
- On 2025-07-15 YouTube renamed its "repetitious content" monetization rule to "inauthentic content"; YouTube called it a minor update with no new policy — [TechRepublic](https://techrepublic.com/article/news-youtube-ai-video-monetization-rules)
- January 2026: 16 channels with a combined 35 million subscribers and 4.7 billion lifetime views were terminated or wiped under that policy (secondary reporting) — [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/news-air/how-youtube-treats-ai-in-2026-new-update)
- 2026-07-16 clarification reportedly named three non-monetizable categories: generic or repetitive content, unsatisfying or off-putting content, and AI personas on sensitive topics (secondary outlets) — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/blog/youtube-updates-july-2026)
- The Next Web (2026-06-15) reports that enforcement is also penalizing faceless creators who never used AI — [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/youtube-ai-slop-crackdown-faceless-creators-collateral-damage)
- Claims of "thousands" of demonetized channels come from small blogs and are unconfirmed — [Milx](https://milx.app/news/why-youtube-just-suspended-thousands-of-ai-channels-and-how-to-protect-yours)

**TikTok**
- Creator Rewards thresholds: 10,000+ followers and 100,000 views in the last 30 days; videos generally over one minute — [Daily Dot](https://dailydot.com/tiktok-creator-rewards-program)
- Sources conflict on whether fully AI-generated videos qualify: one says explicitly prohibited, another says no ban; none is an official TikTok statement (2026) — [Virvid](https://virvid.ai/blog/tiktok-monetization-alternatives-2026-ai-content-ban); contradicted by [Clippie](https://clippie.ai/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-faceless-ai-videos)
- RPM around $0.40-$1.00 per thousand views for well-performing content (single third-party source, 2026) — [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/ai-generated-videos-monetised-tiktok/)
- TikTok Shop banned AI voices from livestreams — [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/?p=3823468)

**AI UGC / avatar ads**
- Arcads: about $5,000/month in early 2024 to $10M ARR roughly 20 months after launch, 6,000+ paying customers as of November 2025, team of eight [SELF-REPORTED] — [Founderpath](https://founderpath.com/resources/romain-torres-arcads-10m-arr-ai-video-ads-sales)
- Arcads then announced a $16M seed led by Eurazeo in December 2025, so "bootstrapped" no longer applies; a $15M figure dated April 2026 is unverified — [Latka](https://getlatka.com/blog/arcads-revenue-ceo)
- Competitors: Creatify above $9M ARR, HeyGen around $100M ARR, MakeUGC bootstrapped with 10,000+ companies (2026) — [Trends.vc](https://trends.vc/synthetic-ugc-ads-ai-actors-managed-agencies-creative-fatigue/)

**Newsletters**
- Top tier (over 1M subscribers, e.g. The Rundown AI, Superhuman AI) estimated at $5M-$15M+/year (third-party estimate, not reported financials, 2026) — [Creative AI News](https://www.creativeainews.com/articles/ai-newsletter-landscape-2026/)
- The Rundown University charges $99/month with 5,000+ active members (2026) — [Built by Foundry](https://www.builtbyfoundry.io/blog/rowan-cheung-the-rundown-1-7m-readers-29-plan-70-percent-expensed)
- Small AI newsletters for sale show the other end: 75,000 subscribers with $1,390 trailing-twelve-month revenue; 23,000 subscribers with $4,330 TTM (seller-reported) — [Microns listing 1](https://microns.io/startup-listings/ai-trends-newsletter-bundle); [Microns listing 2](https://microns.io/startup-listings/newsletter-about-ai-63b3f)

**Programmatic SEO**
- Google's scaled content abuse policy (2024-03-05) applies regardless of whether content is automated or human-made — [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/scaled-content-abuse/)
- One monitor of 49,345 sites reported 837 deindexed within days (about 1.7%, 20.7M monthly visits) — [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/generative-models-drive-scaled-content-abuse-167916bb)
- Case study: a site section with 850K+ AI-generated URLs received a manual action and left the index — [G-Squared Interactive](https://www.gsqi.com/marketing-blog/when-mt-ai-crumbles-chatgpt-follows/)
- Spam updates continued through June and August 2026 — [G-Squared Interactive](https://gsqi.com/marketing-blog/august-2026-google-spam-update-case-studies/)
- John Mueller warned that programmatic SEO sites can lose Google's trust and recovery takes significant time — [TechWyse](https://www.techwyse.com/news/search-seo/google-john-mueller-programmatic-seo-site-trust-recovery)
- A claimed 50-80% traffic loss in two weeks after a March 2026 core update is unverified — [Product Hunt post](https://www.producthunt.com/p/rankfender/your-ai-generated-pages-are-being-de-indexed-here-is-why)

**E-commerce / print-on-demand**
- Etsy's 2026-10-22 rules: bot-generated listings without human involvement are prohibited; AI use must be disclosed; AI-generated patterns, recipes and instructions banned; most listings need at least one unedited photo of the real item, with some print-on-demand exceptions whose scope is unclear — [Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/10/26/etsy-ai-policy/amp/); [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-bans-ai-patterns/)
- FTC cases against "AI-powered" e-commerce passive-income schemes: Ascend Ecom (at least $25M alleged consumer harm), FBA Machine/Passive Scaling (permanent ban, 2025), Click Profit, Empire Holdings (pending), Growth Cave (settlement banning business-opportunity sales) — [FTC business-opportunity case list](https://www.ftc.gov/industry/franchises-business-opportunities-investments); [Frankfurt Kurnit](https://advertisinglaw.fkks.com/post/102kymz/ftc-shuts-down-ai-driven-business-opportunity-scheme-continues-sweep-of-deceptiv)

### Inferences
- Pure-AI, high-volume content is the model with the worst risk-adjusted outlook in the US in 2026: income depends on a single platform that is actively tightening rules.
- The defensible version is service work (producing AI ad creative for paying brands), where the client pays regardless of platform monetization. No verified earnings data for small AI-creative agencies surfaced, though.
- Small newsletters monetize poorly; subscriber count alone does not equal revenue (75,000 subscribers, $1,390/year).
- Requirements: capital very low; skills are editing, niche judgment and distribution; time to monetization on YouTube is gated by Partner Program thresholds. Hype share: very high for faceless channels and AI e-commerce (FTC-confirmed fraud in the latter).

### Gaps
- No verified earnings distributions for faceless channels, AI UGC freelancers or AI influencer accounts.
- Nothing found on Meta's monetization stance or Amazon KDP limits.
- No seller-earnings or saturation data for AI print-on-demand.

## 5. Forward-deployed / "AI roll-up" and services-as-software

### Takeaway
This is a venture- and private-equity-scale strategy, not a low-capital one, and its published results are investor claims rather than audited outcomes. The transferable idea for a small operator is to run a service business with AI-raised margins, without the acquisitions.

### Cited Findings
- General Catalyst set aside $1.5B in late 2023 for AI-enabled roll-ups, spanning seven industries with plans for up to 20 as of September 2025 — [Capital and Clarity](https://capitalandclarity.substack.com/p/the-general-catalyst-behind-15-billion)
- Firm-reported claim: some portfolio companies doubled EBITDA margins within 12 months; stated target is moving margins from 15-20% toward 30-40% by automating 30-70% of repetitive labor (not independently verified) — [AIMultiple](https://aimultiple.com/pt/ai-rollups)
- Named portfolio: Eudia (law), Crescendo (call centers), Titan (IT services, $74M backing, claims up to 38% of typical IT tasks automated) — [Air Street Press](https://press.airstreet.com/p/ai-rollup-mirage-technology-2025)
- Khosla Ventures and other VCs were also experimenting with AI-infused roll-ups (2025-05-23) — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/05/23/khosla-ventures-among-vcs-experimenting-with-ai-infused-roll-ups-of-mature-companies)
- Property management: Dwelly (London) reportedly negotiating roughly $200M in combined equity and debt — [Propmodo](https://propmodo.com/property-management-rollups-attract-hundreds-of-millions-in-ai-driven-capital/)
- Accounting: a tax-return processing agent co-built with OpenAI is used by an accounting roll-up that has acquired dozens of firms; General Catalyst also backs accounting firm Accrual (2026) — [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/?p=4039862)
- In a 2026 survey of 102 AI roll-up investors, more than 90% named a 2x EBITDA improvement as the meaningful bar (a target, not a result) — [Pebblous](https://blog.pebblous.ai/blog/ai-rollup-workflow-records-diligence/en/)
- Critical view: "The AI rollup mirage and the risk of repeating old mistakes"; execution of acquisitions is the pitfall and thin margins leave little room for error — [Air Street Press](https://press.airstreet.com/p/ai-rollup-mirage-technology-2025)

### Inferences
- Not replicable with limited capital as designed. The low-capital analogue is starting or taking over one small service operation (bookkeeping, marketing fulfilment, customer support) and using AI to deliver with fewer people, selling the outcome rather than software.
- Evidence strength: weak. Capital raised is well documented; realized margin gains are not.

### Gaps
- No audited results for any AI roll-up.
- Nothing found on Crete Professionals Alliance, Long Lake or search-fund-scale AI roll-ups.
- No documented small-operator (sub-$1M) cases.

## 6. AI education, training and community businesses

### Takeaway
Selling AI education is demonstrably large at the top, and the biggest reported incomes in the "AI agency" world come from teaching it, not from doing it. Median community income is small.

### Cited Findings
- AI Automation Society (Skool): about 121.7K members; its Plus tier is $129/month with about 3,700 members (2026) — [Joe Apfelbaum](https://joeapfelbaum.substack.com/p/skool-communities-analysis-top-1000); [AI Funnel Insider](https://aifunnelinsider.com/skool-community-price-index/)
- "AI Automation (A-Z)" on Skool: about 164,744 members at $7/month as of 2026-07-14 — [RevenueGeeks](https://revenuegeeks.com/software/skool/best-communities)
- Liam Ottley's AI Automation Agency Hub: 255,000+ members, free course with 60+ hours (user-written post, unverified) — [Typeshare](https://typeshare.co/zacharyauslander320/posts/from-e-commerce-to-ai-empire-how-one-founder-built-a-255k-member-community-by-giving-everything-away-free-n2xa9)
- Nick Saraev: claimed $300K+/month from a paid AI-automation-agency community (newsletter claim, no primary financial source) — [TGC Report](https://tgc-report.beehiiv.com/p/new-post-f7fed4a4074bfa1e)
- Median paid Skool community: about $1,217/month MRR, just over half above $1,000 (third-party scrape estimate) — [Profitable.app](https://profitable.app/skool/stats); a different unverified dataset of the top 1,000 gives median $4,870 and mean $25,707 and notes AI automation communities have high membership but low average MRR — [skool-paid-report](https://skool-paid-report.pages.dev/data/executive_synthesis.md)
- Skool company revenue estimates conflict: $26.6M (Latka) versus about $49.8M for 2026 (CompWorth/CB Insights via secondary source) — [Latka](https://getlatka.com/companies/skool.com/vs/take2); [CommuniPass](https://communipass.com/?p=21073)
- Corporate AI training for small US teams: $2,000-$15,000 per workshop; follow-on custom automation $25,000-$150,000 (vendor guides, 2026) — [SF AI Labs](https://sfailabs.com/guides/how-much-does-corporate-ai-training-cost-in-california-in-2026)
- Enterprise programs: $500-$5,000 per employee, $50,000-$500,000+ per program; budgets said to be up about 40% year over year, attributed to Deloitte but not verified — [iTechnicallyKan](https://itechnicallykan.com/blog/posts/how-much-does-corporate-ai-training-cost/)

### Inferences
- Simple arithmetic on the cited figures (my calculation, not a reported number): 3,700 members at $129 implies roughly $477K/month gross for one top community, if the member count is accurate and all pay full price.
- The structure is a classic "sell shovels to shovel-sellers" pyramid of attention: revenue depends on an existing audience (YouTube), which a newcomer lacks.
- Corporate AI training is the least hyped item here: buyers are companies with budgets, pricing is per workshop, and it works as a lead-in to implementation work. Requirements: near-zero capital, real teaching skill and credibility, a B2B network.
- Regulatory exposure: selling an income opportunity with earnings claims is what the FTC pursued in Air AI and the e-commerce cases.

### Gaps
- No verified revenue for any named community owner.
- No independent survey of small-business demand for AI training.

## 7. Which models are saturated or declining versus still growing (US, 2026)

### Takeaway
Declining or structurally squeezed: mass-produced AI content, programmatic AI SEO, AI print-on-demand, thin-wrapper SaaS and generic low-ticket automation agencies. Growing on the evidence available: voice AI infrastructure usage, AI ad-creative tooling, vertical products with distribution, and paid AI education at the top end.

### Cited Findings
- Growth signal, voice: Retell up 650% year over year to about $60M annualized (April 2026) — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/retell-ai-60m-yr-up-650-yoy)
- Growth signal, AI ad creative: Arcads $10M ARR in about 20 months; HeyGen about $100M ARR — [Founderpath](https://founderpath.com/resources/romain-torres-arcads-10m-arr-ai-video-ads-sales); [Trends.vc](https://trends.vc/synthetic-ugc-ads-ai-actors-managed-agencies-creative-fatigue/)
- Saturation signal, micro-SaaS: median AI startup on TrustMRR about $7 MRR (2026) — [BigIdeasDB](https://bigideasdb.com/docs/trustmrr-overview)
- Saturation signal, agencies: setup fees discounted to $0-$1 (2026) — [Ciela](https://ciela.ai/blogs/how-much-to-charge-for-ai-voice-agent)
- Decline signal, AI content: YouTube terminations (January 2026) and July 2026 clarification — [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/news-air/how-youtube-treats-ai-in-2026-new-update)
- Decline signal, AI SEO: manual actions and repeated 2026 spam updates — [G-Squared Interactive](https://gsqi.com/marketing-blog/august-2026-google-spam-update-case-studies/)
- Decline signal, AI marketplaces: Etsy's October 2026 rules — [Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/10/26/etsy-ai-policy/amp/)
- Marketplace view: average multiples flat in H1 2026, with the best assets in each category pulling far ahead (Flippa EMEA director) — [FE International](https://feinternational.com/blog/tech-ma-mid-year-2026-report)
- Enforcement trend: FTC actions against AI-branded business opportunities continued into 2026 — [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/03/air-ai-its-owners-will-be-banned-marketing-business-opportunities-settle-ftc-charges-company-misled)

### Inferences
Summary table for the report writer (ranges are from the sources above; "evidence" is my grading):

| Model | Capital to start | Core skill | Realistic range for a small operator | Evidence | Hype share |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI automation agency | Under ~$300/month tools | B2B sales, workflow tools | $300-$2,000/month per client; totals unverified | Weak | Very high |
| White-label voice/receptionist | $300-$2,000/month platform | Local sales | $250-$500/month per client, 50-70% claimed margin | Weak (vendor only) | Very high |
| AI micro-SaaS | API and hosting costs | Building plus distribution | Median near $0; top few $100K+/month | Best available | Medium |
| Faceless AI video | Near zero | Editing, niche choice | Unknown; platform-dependent | Weak, risk verified | Very high |
| AI ad creative service | Tool subscriptions | Performance-ad craft | No verified data for small agencies | Weak | Medium |
| AI newsletter | Near zero | Writing, audience growth | Small ones about $1K-$4K/year; giants $5M+ | Mixed | Medium |
| Programmatic AI SEO | Low | SEO | Declining; deindexing risk | Risk verified | High |
| AI roll-up | Millions and up | M&A, operations | Not low-capital | Weak (investor claims) | High |
| AI training (corporate) | Near zero | Teaching, credibility | $2,000-$15,000 per workshop | Vendor pricing only | Low to medium |
| AI community/course | Near zero plus audience | Audience building | Median about $1.2K/month; top several $100K/month | Third-party estimates | High |

- For a capital-constrained replicator, the models with the best ratio of evidence to risk are B2B service work paid by the client (implementation, voice/reception for a specific vertical, corporate training), because revenue does not depend on platform monetization policy. This is a judgment from the pattern of evidence, not something any source states.
- A model's growth in the US at the platform level (voice minutes, ad-creative tools) does not show that small resellers capture that growth.

### Gaps
- No longitudinal data on any model's small-operator population, so "saturated" rests on indirect signals (price pressure, medians, policy changes).
- n8n ARR/valuation and partner-ecosystem data were not retrieved.
- a16z writing on services-as-software was not retrieved.
